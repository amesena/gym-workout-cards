# Training Frequency

Training frequency refers to how often a muscle group is trained within a given microcycle (usually a 7-day period) (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 86).

## Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) Window

Following a resistance training session, MPS elevates significantly within 2–4 hours, peaks at ~24 hours, and returns to baseline within 36–48 hours in trained individuals (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 86; Ch. 1, p. 7).
- Training a muscle **2 times per week** maintains a more continuous state of positive net protein balance compared to training a muscle only once per week (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 87).

## Volume Distribution Vehicle

When total weekly [[concepts/training-volume]] is equalized, meta-analytic data shows that training a muscle group 2+ times per week yields slightly superior growth compared to 1 time per week (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 87).
- **Per-Session Quality**: Distributing 16 weekly sets across 2 sessions (8 sets/session) maintains higher quality effort, rep speed, and lower intra-session fatigue than performing 16 sets in a single session.

## Practical Frequency Guidelines

- **Beginners**: 2–3 full-body sessions per week.
- **Intermediate/Advanced**: 2x/week per muscle group via upper/lower splits, push/pull/legs, or specialized splits (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 88).
