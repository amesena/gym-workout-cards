# Repetition Tempo

Repetition tempo specifies the duration (in seconds) assigned to each phase of a repetition: concentric, peak contraction isometric, eccentric, and bottom stretch isometric (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 89).

## Optimal Velocity Range

Meta-analyses by Schoenfeld conclude that **repetition durations between 0.5 and 6 seconds produce equivalent muscle hypertrophy** when sets are driven to near failure (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, pp. 89–90).
- **Super-Slow Tempos (>10s per rep)**: Inferior for hypertrophy because external load must be severely reduced, diminishing total [[concepts/mechanical-tension]] per fiber (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 90).

## Practical Cadence Recommendations

- **Concentric Phase**: Perform fast or intentionally explosive (1–2 seconds) to maximize motor unit recruitment.
- **Eccentric Phase**: Perform under control (2–3 seconds) to maintain mechanical tension throughout muscle lengthening (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, p. 91).
