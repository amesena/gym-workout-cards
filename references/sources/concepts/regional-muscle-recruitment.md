# Regional Muscle Recruitment

Regional muscle recruitment involves manipulating exercise geometry to alter muscle line-of-pull, selectively emphasizing specific muscle heads or fiber regions (Delavier, pp. 8–180; [[concepts/exercise-selection]]).

## Major Compartmentalization Examples

1. **Pectoralis Major**:
   - **Incline Press (30–45°)**: Emphasizes the **clavicular head** (upper chest) due to increased shoulder flexion angle (Delavier, p. 64).
   - **Decline Press / Dips**: Emphasizes the **sternocostal & abdominal heads** (lower chest) (Delavier, pp. 66–68).
2. **Triceps Brachii**:
   - **Overhead Triceps Extension**: Places the **long head** under stretch due to shoulder flexion, optimizing its recruitment (Delavier, p. 22).
   - **Triceps Pushdown (Pronated)**: Emphasizes the **lateral and medial heads** (Delavier, p. 20).
3. **Latissimus Dorsi & Trapezius**:
   - **Wide-Grip Lat Pulldowns**: Emphasizes upper/outer latissimus fibers and teres major (Delavier, p. 86).
   - **Close-Grip / Supinated Pulls**: Emphasizes lower latissimus fibers and biceps brachii (Delavier, p. 88).
   - **Heavy Shrugs**: Targets upper trapezius, whereas horizontal rows emphasize middle trapezius and rhomboids (Delavier, pp. 92, 104).
4. **Quadriceps Femoris**:
   - **Narrow Stance Squats**: Shifts focus onto the **vastus lateralis** (Delavier, p. 126).
   - **Wide Stance / Feet Turn-Out**: Increases involvement of the **vastus medialis** and adductors (Delavier, p. 128).

## Practical Application

Integrating complementary movement angles prevents regional muscular imbalances and ensures complete 3D muscle development (Delavier, pp. 7, 61).
