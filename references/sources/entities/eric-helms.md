# Eric Helms

Eric Helms, PhD, CSCS, is an exercise scientist, research fellow at AUT University, chief coach at 3D Muscle Journey (3DMJ), competitive natural bodybuilder, and powerlifter.

## Contributions to Strength & Hypertrophy Science

- Creator of [[references/the-muscle-and-strength-pyramid-training]], establishing the 6-level hierarchy of training priorities.
- Published extensive research on RPE/RIR autoregulation in resistance training, macronutrient manipulation for physique athletes, and protein timing during contest prep (Helms et al., pp. 45–50).
- Pioneer in translating evidence-based research into practical coaching frameworks for natural bodybuilders and powerlifters.
