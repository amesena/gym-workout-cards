# Frédéric Delavier

Frédéric Delavier is a French artist, author, and researcher in exercise anatomy and biomechanics. He studied morphology and anatomy at the École des Beaux-Arts in Paris and dissected specimens at the Faculté de Médecine.

## Key Contributions

- Author and illustrator of [[references/strength-training-anatomy]], one of the best-selling strength training books globally.
- Pioneer in visualizing functional muscle contraction, joint articulation, and injury risks during resistance exercises.
- Investigated morphological differences (e.g., limb length ratios, muscle bellies) affecting biomechanical efficiency in strength training (Delavier, pp. 4–6).
