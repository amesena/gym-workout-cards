# Brad Schoenfeld

Brad Schoenfeld, PhD, CSCS, CSPS, FNSCA, is a internationally renowned exercise scientist, professor of exercise science at Lehman College, and head of the Human Performance Laboratory.

## Contributions to Hypertrophy Science

Schoenfeld has published hundreds of peer-reviewed studies investigating resistance training adaptations. His key contributions include:
- Establishing the dose-response relationship of [[concepts/training-volume]] for muscle growth (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, pp. 83–86).
- Investigating the hypertrophic equivalence of high-load vs low-load resistance training when sets are taken to near [[concepts/proximity-to-failure]] (Schoenfeld, Ch. 4, pp. 78–82).
- Clarifying the molecular mechanisms of [[concepts/mechanical-tension]], [[concepts/metabolic-stress]], and [[concepts/muscle-damage]] (Schoenfeld, Ch. 2, pp. 29–46).
- Authoring [[references/science-and-development-of-muscle-hypertrophy]], a standard reference textbook in exercise physiology.
