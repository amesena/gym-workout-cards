# G. Gregory Haff

G. Gregory Haff, PhD, CSCS,*D, FNSCA, is a internationally recognized strength scientist, Professor and Course Coordinator for Postgraduate Strength and Conditioning at Edith Cowan University, and past President of the National Strength and Conditioning Association ([[entities/nsca]]).

## Scientific Contributions

- Lead editor of the 4th Edition of [[references/essentials-of-strength-training-and-conditioning]].
- Extensively published on periodization models, rate of force development (RFD), isometric mid-thigh pull (IMTP) testing, and microcycle volume loading in elite athletes.
